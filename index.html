A private publishing tool for the RM7 Setups YouTube channel

RM7 Setups makes car setups for sim racing games (Le Mans Ultimate and iRacing) and publishes hotlap and setup guide videos on its YouTube channel.

RM7 Studio is a desktop application that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It automates the repetitive publishing work around each video:

  • creating the video thumbnail from a screenshot and a template,
  • writing the title, description and tags from fixed templates,
  • uploading the video and its thumbnail to the RM7 Setups channel as private, and adding it to the right playlists.

The channel owner then reviews every video in YouTube Studio and publishes it by hand.

Who can use it?

RM7 Studio is not a public product and is not distributed. It is used by a single person, the owner of the RM7 Setups channel, and it only ever connects to that one YouTube channel.

Use of YouTube and Google data

RM7 Studio uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set thumbnails and manage playlists on the owner's channel. It does not collect, sell or share any data. Read the details in the Privacy Policy.
